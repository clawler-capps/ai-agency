Each delivery step, with timing

From walk-in to retainer

1

Free walk-in assessment 15 min + report in ~1 min

  • Start any notetaker. Tell them: “I'm just going to record this so I don't forget.” (transcript)
  • Talk for 15 minutes about pain points ("try and twist on them") and which software they use. (transcript)
  • The transcript goes to a simple n8n / Make.com automation. AI picks the top opportunities and fills in a premade template. (transcript)
  • Hit send while standing in front of them. They have the report within a minute. (transcript)
  • Corey: this removes the usual "call, research, second call" gap. “Time kills all deals.” (transcript)
2

Upsell to the deeper assessment 1 hr meeting + 1–2 days (up to a week) to deliver

  • Pitch: "That was 15 minutes. We barely skimmed your business. We have a paid upgrade." (transcript)
  • For early clients they did it free in exchange for honest feedback, framed openly: "I'm starting out." (transcript)
  • Take a day or two before delivering. Corey: an instant report on a paid assessment makes the client wonder why they paid $1K. (transcript)
  • Goal: quote them on becoming their "AI department." (transcript)
3

Enterprise assessment ($10M+ companies) ~7.5 hrs of interviews + build time

ROI-math method

Value = hours saved × wage + throughput gains

Lever 1: saved hours

Hours saved × what they pay those employees. (transcript)

Lever 2: throughput (the big one)

More revenue-generating activity, like more proposals out the door at their real win rate and gross margin. (transcript)

Stay conservative

Under-promise, over-deliver. Say so out loud: "we'd hate to tell you one thing and not deliver." Clients appreciated it. (transcript)

Systems scale with the business

Corey: estimates are a snapshot. Grow 20% and a $255K year-one value could be ~$300K in year two. (transcript)

The unquantified upside

Putting the team on Claude/Codex connected to all their software has no clean ROI number, but they "understood the magnitude." There's also quality-of-life and mental bandwidth. (transcript)

Their published guarantee

"The workflows we build pay for our fee within the first year," tracked on a monthly dashboard. source: certifiedaipartners.com/pricing

Our take · reusable formula

Annual value = (hrs saved/task × tasks/yr × loaded wage) + (extra units × win rate × gross profit/unit)

  • Use the client's own numbers from the interviews. Say "you told us…" to make the figures defensible.
  • Cap throughput lift at 10% unless they give you a higher number.
  • Show a year-one figure that accounts for ramp-up and a separate steady-state figure, as James did.
  • This matches our site's "10× the fee" promise, and the same math can feed our Leverage Map. source: components/ServiceTiers.tsx

30-day action plan

Everything on this tab is Our take unless tagged otherwise. It builds on our current $997 assessment, the $15K/mo Embedded rung, and the GHL setup.

Week 1 · Days 1–7 · Plumbing

  • Connect /assessment to GHL (webhook → workflow → contact + tag + email). It currently loses every lead. source: repo doc
  • Build the 15-min walk-in flow: notetaker → GHL/n8n → Claude → branded report emailed in ~1 min.
  • Create a GHL pipeline: Free mini → $997 assessment → Company assessment → C-suite readout → Proposal → Retainer.
  • Write the ICP: Sacramento-region companies doing $10M+ that just went from small to mid-size.
  • "Who do I already know?" List 50 people who could introduce you to that ICP.

Week 2 · Days 8–14 · Walk-ins & chambers

  • Walk into 20 offices: CPA firms, dental, orthopedic (James's list), plus home-service and construction offices.
  • Run free 15-min assessments. Offer 2–3 of them a free deeper version in exchange for feedback and a testimonial.
  • Email the chambers offering a free AI talk: Sacramento Metro Chamber, Roseville, Folsom, Elk Grove, Rancho Cordova, El Dorado Hills, Sacramento Hispanic Chamber, North Sacramento. source: localwiki.org/sac, eldoradohillschamber.org
  • Send 10 warm-intro / advice asks from the Week-1 list.

Week 3 · Days 15–21 · Upmarket

  • Pull 50 C-suite contacts at Sacramento $10M+ companies (company websites, LinkedIn, treg.to trial).
  • Send the advice-ask email plus a LinkedIn connect to each. Goal: 5 advice calls.
  • Build the company-assessment kit in Claude Code: pre-call survey that forces a 30-min booking, interview guide, Leverage Map template, demo scaffold.
  • Make a chamber talk deck with a QR code to /assessment.

Week 4 · Days 22–30 · Sell the big one

  • Give the first chamber talk. Tag attendees in GHL. Follow up within 24 hrs.
  • Pitch the first company assessment at $5K–$10K, credited to month one.
  • Prep a 3-tier proposal ($15K / $25K / $40K per month, $5K maintenance floor).
  • Line up a builder: a Sacramento State or UC Davis CS student, as James did with BYU.
  • Review: walk-ins, minis sent, $997s sold, advice calls, assessments in pipeline.

Our take · how this fits what we have
  • $997 assessment stays as the small-business product and the walk-in upsell. Don't discount it. Use free-for-feedback only for the first 2–3.
  • Add the company-wide assessment as a bridge from $997 to the $15K/mo Embedded Partner.
